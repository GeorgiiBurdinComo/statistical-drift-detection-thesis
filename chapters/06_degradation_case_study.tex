\section{Transient versus sustained degradation}
\label{sec:transient-provider-degradation}

A drop in monitored accuracy does not necessarily indicate a permanent model failure. In practice, we observed cases where model performance temporarily degraded due to provider-side factors such as routing changes or infrastructure issues. For example, GPT-4.1 Mini experienced a significant drop in performance—falling even below Nano-level quality—on March 22--23, before recovering to its previous level after a few days.

In our case, this did not require intervention since the affected model was not in active use. However, this highlights a non-obvious but important phenomenon: short-term degradation can resolve without action. Therefore, when a production model shows performance decline, it may be reasonable to first switch to a fallback model while continuing to monitor, anticipating possible recovery. Only if the degradation persists should more permanent prompt optimization be considered.
